The yUSDS system is currently in development; its elements are outlined in the subdocuments below.
                
                
                During the transition period to the yUSDS system, the protocol-dependent SKY-backed borrowing system remains operational and is governed by A.4.5 - Short Term Transitionary Measures.
                
                - **yUSDS Function** (*Core*) - yUSDS is a yield-bearing token representing USDS deposited into the yUSDS contract. Its primary function is to provide segregated risk capital for the SKY-backed borrowing mechanism. Users convert USDS to yUSDS to provide this capital and earn the yUSDS Rate. See A.4.5 - yUSDS Rate. yUSDS can be converted back to USDS subject to available unutilized liquidity in the yUSDS converter contract.
                - **yUSDS Rate** *(Core)* - The variable yield earned by yUSDS holders is calculated using the formula:
                    
                    
                    `yUSDS Rate = Utilization * (SKY Borrow Rate - yUSDS Accessibility Reward) + (1 - Utilization) * Sky Savings Rate`
                    
                    Depositors earn a rate derived from the SKY Borrow Rate less the yUSDS Accessibility Reward on the utilized portion of their capital and the Sky Savings Rate on the unutilized portion.
                    
                    - **Parameters Definition** *(Core)* - The parameters of the yUSDS Rate formula are further defined in the documents herein.

                        - **SKY Borrow Rate Definition** *(Core)* - `SKY Borrow Rate` is the SKY Borrow Rate defined in A.4.5 - SKY Borrow Rate.
                        - **Sky Savings Rate Definition** *(Core)* - `Sky Savings Rate` is the Sky Savings Rate defined in [A.3.2 - Core Stability Parameters - Parameters - Sky Savings Rate](https://sky-atlas.powerhouse.io/A.3.2.2.3_Sky_Savings_Rate/73d91126-21c9-4e8a-bedf-8a51a432bb59|57eab733e951).
                        - **yUSDS Accessibility Reward Definition** *(Core)* - `yUSDS Accessibility Reward` is defined in A.4.5 - yUSDS Accessibility Reward.
                - **yUSDS Holders’ Risk Bearing** *(Core)* - Holders of yUSDS accept the risk associated with providing capital for SKY-backed borrowing. In the event that the liquidation of a borrower’s staked SKY collateral does not cover the outstanding debt, yUSDS balances will be subject to a haircut proportional to the shortfall. This mechanism ensures that the Sky Protocol is isolated from losses originating from the SKY-backed borrowing facility.
                - **Debt Ceiling** (*Core*) - The maximum amount of USDS that can be borrowed against staked SKY is dynamically determined by, and equal to, the total amount of USDS currently held within the yUSDS contract. This dynamic ceiling will replace the static `DC-IAM` module and parameters associated with protocol-dependent, SKY-backed borrowing.
                - **SKY Borrow Rate** *(Core)* - The interest rate charged to borrowers (SKY Borrow Rate) is dynamic and market-driven, based on the utilization of funds within the yUSDS contract. This dynamic rate will replace the static `Stability Fee` parameter associated with protocol-dependent, SKY-backed borrowing.
                    - **Rate Setting Mechanism** *(Core)* - The SKY Borrow Rate adjusts to target a 90% utilization rate of the USDS in the yUSDS contract. When yUSDS utilization is below 90%, the rate gradually decreases; when above 90%, it gradually increases. The SKY Borrow Rate cannot fall below the SKY Borrow Minimum Rate. See A.4.5 - SKY Borrow Minimum Rate.
                        
                        The specific parameters and formula governing the rate of adjustment will be specified in a future iteration of the Atlas.
                        
                    - **SKY Borrow Minimum Rate** *(Core)* - The SKY Borrow Minimum Rate is calculated according to the following formula:
                        
                        
                        `SKY Borrow Minimum Rate = Sky Savings Rate + yUSDS Accessibility Reward` 
                        
                        The formula ensures the SKY Borrow Minimum Rate covers the baseline cost of capital (Sky Savings Rate) plus the incentive cost (yUSDS Accessibility Reward), preventing value-draining arbitrage where users could borrow below the Sky Savings Rate.
